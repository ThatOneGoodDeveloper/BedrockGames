Tetris Unblocked — game guide

Tetris needs no introduction, but it does deserve a good port. The version on this page follows modern guideline rules: a seven-bag randomizer so you never wait too long for a straight piece, a hold slot for saving a piece for later, a three-piece next queue, a ghost piece showing where your block will land, and proper wall kicks when you rotate against the edge.

The result is a game that feels fair at high speed. Clearing one line is easy, clearing four at once — a Tetris — is where the scoring explodes. The level counter rises as you clear lines, and each level nudges the fall speed up until the board becomes a blur of decisions.

Keyboard is the primary control scheme, but touch buttons are included for phones and tablets. Your best score is saved locally, and the game pauses automatically if you switch tabs.

How to play Tetris

  1. Use the left and right arrows to move the falling piece.
  2. Press the up arrow or X to rotate it clockwise, and Z to rotate counter-clockwise.
  3. Press Down for a soft drop, or Space for an instant hard drop.
  4. Fill an entire horizontal row to clear it and earn points.
  5. Clear four rows at once for a Tetris bonus and a big score boost.

Tips and strategy

  • Keep the board flat: a single deep well is easier to clear than several shallow holes.
  • Save the long straight piece with the hold slot when your well is almost ready.
  • Use the ghost piece to line up hard drops instead of guessing.
  • Do not rotate pieces at the last second near the stack — wall kicks can surprise you.

Tetris FAQ

Does this Tetris follow official guideline rules?

It uses the core guideline ideas: a seven-bag randomizer, hold, ghost piece, three-piece preview and basic wall kicks. It is a clean fan recreation rather than an official licensed release.

Can I play Tetris unblocked on a school Chromebook?

Yes. The game runs entirely in the browser with HTML5 canvas and JavaScript, with no plugins, downloads or installs.

How does scoring work in Tetris?

Single, double, triple and quadruple line clears award increasing points, multiplied by the current level. Soft and hard drops also add a small amount.

Is there a two-player mode?

This version is single-player with a saved high score. It focuses on a polished solo experience with hold and preview support.
