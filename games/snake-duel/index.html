Snake Duel Unblocked — game guide

Snake Duel takes the classic Snake rules and makes them competitive. Two snakes share the same grid, the same food, and the same shrinking amount of safe space. Eat, grow, and force the other player into a wall, a body or a head-on collision.

Player 1 controls the green snake with W A S D; Player 2 controls the pink snake with the arrow keys. On a phone or tablet, each player gets their own on-screen pad. The round ends as soon as one snake crashes, and the scoreboard keeps track of round wins.

The strategy is about zoning. Cutting off the center of the board is stronger than chasing food, and a short snake can turn more tightly than a long one. If you are behind on length, use the edges to force a head-on collision you can survive.

How to play Snake Duel

  1. Player 1 uses W A S D; Player 2 uses the arrow keys.
  2. Eat the red food to grow and score a point.
  3. Do not hit the walls, your own body or the other snake.
  4. A head-on collision or a double crash ends the round in a draw.
  5. The surviving snake wins the round; the first to the session target keeps bragging rights.

Tips and strategy

  • Control the center early; it gives you more escape routes.
  • Do not chase food into a corner when the other snake is close.
  • A longer snake turns worse — use that against your opponent.
  • When trapped, aim for a head-on collision if you are shorter.

Snake Duel FAQ

Can two people play Snake Duel on one keyboard?

Yes. Player 1 uses W A S D and Player 2 uses the arrow keys. Both snakes move at the same speed.

Does Snake Duel work on a phone?

Yes. On touch devices, each player gets a separate on-screen direction pad at the bottom of the screen.

What happens if both snakes crash?

The round is a draw. Start a new round with the R key or the restart button.

Can I play Snake Duel unblocked at school?

Yes. It is a static HTML5 game with no downloads or accounts and works on Chromebooks and restricted networks.
