Penalty Shootout Unblocked — game guide

Penalty Shootout compresses football into its most tense moment: the ball on the spot, the keeper on the line, and a whole shootout resting on where you place the next kick. You get five attempts to score more than the computer.

Aim with the mouse or your finger, then use the power meter to decide between a careful placement and a rocket into the corner. The keeper dives based on a mix of reading your aim and a little randomness, so no two shootouts feel the same. Sudden death kicks in if the scores are level after five rounds.

It is quick, replayable, and surprisingly tense for a two-minute game. The best shootout score is saved locally.

How to play Penalty Shootout

  1. Move the crosshair to choose where to aim.
  2. Hold the mouse button or your finger to charge the power meter.
  3. Release to strike the ball.
  4. Score more goals than the computer after five rounds to win.
  5. If the score is tied, the shootout goes to sudden death.

Tips and strategy

  • Aim for the top corners — they are the hardest places for a keeper to reach.
  • Do not max out the power bar every time; placement beats raw power.
  • Watch the keeper before you shoot to see which way they are leaning.
  • Stay calm in sudden death; the same rules apply.

Penalty Shootout FAQ

How many rounds are in a Penalty Shootout?

The main shootout is five rounds per side. If the score is tied after five, it continues into sudden death until one side misses.

Can I play Penalty Shootout unblocked at school?

Yes. It is a static HTML5 game with no downloads or plugins and runs on Chromebooks and restricted networks.

Does the goalkeeper learn my patterns?

The keeper mixes a read of your aim with randomness, so repeating the same corner is risky but not automatically punished every time.

Does it work on a phone?

Yes. Aim and shoot entirely with touch: drag to aim and hold to charge power.
