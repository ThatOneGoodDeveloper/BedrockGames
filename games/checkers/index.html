Checkers Unblocked — game guide

Checkers is one of the oldest strategy games in the world, and it still works because the rules fit in a sentence while the tactics do not. Pieces move diagonally, captures are mandatory, and reaching the far side crowns a king that can move in both directions.

This version supports local two-player games and a minimax computer at three difficulty levels. The board highlights every legal destination, captured pieces are removed with a satisfying hit, and the scoreboard tracks wins for the session.

The most common mistake is treating a free capture as automatically good. A jump can pull your piece away from the defence of the back row, open a lane for the opponent, or walk into a two-for-one. Look at the whole board before you take the bait.

How to play Checkers

  1. Select one of your pieces, then click a highlighted square.
  2. Move diagonally forward one square, or jump over an opponent to capture.
  3. Captures are mandatory when one is available.
  4. Reach the far row to crown a king that can move backwards.
  5. Win by capturing every opponent piece or leaving them with no legal move.

Tips and strategy

  • Keep your back row intact for as long as possible.
  • Trade pieces when you are ahead; simplify when you are winning.
  • Force captures that lead your piece toward the crowning row.
  • Watch for two-for-one jumps before you move a defender.

Checkers FAQ

Can two people play Checkers on one device?

Yes. Choose two-player mode and share the mouse or touchscreen. Red moves first.

Are captures mandatory in this version?

Yes. If a capture is available, only capturing moves are shown. This is the standard international draughts rule.

How strong is the computer?

The CPU uses minimax with alpha-beta pruning. Easy looks one move ahead, medium three, and hard five, with a piece and advancement score.

Can I play Checkers unblocked at school?

Yes. It is a static HTML5 game with no downloads or accounts and runs on Chromebooks and restricted networks.
