Breakout Unblocked — game guide

Breakout is the original "one more brick" game. A ball bounces around the screen, you slide a paddle underneath it, and every brick you hit disappears. Clear the wall and a fresh, tougher one appears.

This version adds a little modern sparkle without changing the formula: particle bursts when bricks break, three power-ups that can turn a losing run around, and a level system that introduces tougher multi-hit bricks as you progress. The ball speeds up slightly with each level, so the challenge keeps climbing.

It is playable with a mouse, a finger, or the arrow keys. If you are on a trackpad, the mouse control is the most precise; if you are on a phone, dragging the paddle directly feels the most natural.

How to play Breakout

  1. Move the paddle left and right to keep the ball in play.
  2. Press Space to launch the ball at the start of a round.
  3. Hit every brick to clear the level and move on.
  4. Catch falling power-ups to gain extra balls, a wider paddle or a slower ball.
  5. Do not let the ball fall past the paddle — you lose a life.

Tips and strategy

  • Hit the ball with the edge of the paddle to change its angle sharply.
  • Aim for the sides of the wall first; the ball can get trapped up top and clear rows quickly.
  • Save the wide-paddle power-up for levels with narrow gaps.
  • Do not chase the ball — move the paddle to where the ball will land.

Breakout FAQ

What power-ups are in this Breakout game?

There are three: a multi-ball drop that adds extra balls, a wide-paddle drop, and a slow-ball drop that reduces the ball speed for a short time.

Can I play Breakout unblocked on a Chromebook?

Yes. The game is pure HTML5 canvas and JavaScript with no downloads or plugins, and it runs well on low-power school devices.

How do I control the paddle on a phone?

Drag your finger left and right anywhere in the game area. The paddle follows your touch directly, with no lag.

Does Breakout save my score?

Yes. Your best score is stored in localStorage on your device and shown in the game toolbar.
