Frogger Unblocked — game guide

Frogger is a game about timing, not speed. One hop in the wrong direction puts you in front of a car or into the river. The safe route changes every second as traffic and logs move across the screen, so the correct move is always relative to the lane you are about to enter.

This version has five road lanes, five river lanes, moving logs, five homes at the top and a level system that speeds everything up. Landing on a log carries you with it; missing the log means a splash. Filling all five homes advances the level.

The classic strategy still applies: move one lane at a time, wait for a gap, and treat the median as a reset point. On the river, aim for the center of a log rather than the edge, and remember that a log can carry you off the screen if you stay on it too long.

How to play Frogger

  1. Use the arrow keys or WASD to hop one square at a time.
  2. Cross the road without touching a car.
  3. Ride the logs across the river — do not fall into the water.
  4. Reach one of the five homes at the top to score.
  5. Fill all five homes to advance to a faster level.

Tips and strategy

  • Move one lane at a time and wait for a clear gap.
  • Aim for the center of a log, not the edge.
  • Use the median as a safe place to pause and read the traffic.
  • Do not rush the last lane; cars are faster than they look.

Frogger FAQ

Is this the original Frogger?

It is an original HTML5 recreation of the road-and-river crossing formula, with new art, lane layouts and level progression.

Can I play Frogger unblocked at school?

Yes. It is a static HTML5 game with no downloads or plugins, built for Chromebooks and restricted networks.

What happens when I fill all five homes?

The level advances, the traffic and logs speed up, and you get a score bonus. The layout is rebuilt for the new level.

Does Frogger work on mobile?

Yes. Swipe or use the on-screen D-pad to hop. The board scales to fit the screen.
