Blackjack Unblocked — game guide

Blackjack is the card game where the rules are simple but the decisions are not. Get closer to 21 than the dealer without going over. Aces count as 1 or 11, face cards count as 10, and the dealer must stand on 17.

This version uses a real 52-card deck, free chips and the three decisions that matter: hit, stand, or double. Doubling doubles your bet, gives you exactly one more card, and then hands the turn to the dealer. Blackjack pays 3:2; a normal win pays even money; a push returns your bet.

The bankroll is saved locally, so you can come back to the same chip count. There is no real money, no purchase and no account. If the bankroll drops below the table minimum, it resets to 100 chips so you can keep playing.

How to play Blackjack

  1. Press New round to deal two cards to yourself and two to the dealer.
  2. Hit to take another card; stand to end your turn.
  3. Double to double your bet, take one card, and stand automatically.
  4. The dealer reveals the hole card and draws until reaching 17.
  5. Beat the dealer without going over 21 to win the hand.

Tips and strategy

  • Stand on 17 or higher; the risk of busting is not worth it.
  • Hit on 11 or lower — you cannot bust with one card.
  • Double when you have 10 or 11 and the dealer shows a weak card.
  • Aces are flexible: count them as 11 until that would bust you.

Blackjack FAQ

Is Blackjack free to play?

Yes. The chips are virtual and have no cash value. There are no purchases, accounts or real-money gambling on this page.

Does the dealer hit or stand on soft 17?

The dealer stands on all 17s, including soft 17, in this version. That is slightly more player-friendly than a hit-soft-17 table.

Can I play Blackjack unblocked at school?

Yes. It is a static HTML5 card game with no downloads or accounts and runs on Chromebooks and restricted networks.

Is my bankroll saved?

Yes. Your chip count is stored in localStorage on your device. Clearing site data resets it.
