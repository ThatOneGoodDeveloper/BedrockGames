Flappy Bird Unblocked — game guide

Flappy Bird became a phenomenon for one reason: it is brutally, hilariously simple. A single tap lifts the bird. Gravity pulls it back down. Between you and the next point is a pair of pipes with a gap that always looks slightly smaller than it is.

The game on this page recreates that loop with a clean parallax skyline, smooth physics and the medal system from the original: bronze at 10, silver at 20, gold at 30 and platinum at 40. A saved best score gives you something to chase after the first ten attempts inevitably end in a pipe.

It is also a genuinely good test of rhythm. Rather than mashing, try to find a steady tap cadence that keeps the bird level with the middle of each gap. That single habit is the difference between a score of 4 and a score of 40.

How to play Flappy Bird

  1. Press Space, click, or tap the screen to make the bird flap.
  2. Pass through the gap between each pair of pipes to score a point.
  3. Do not touch the pipes, the ground or the top of the screen.
  4. Keep a steady tapping rhythm instead of mashing the button.
  5. Collect medals at 10, 20, 30 and 40 points.

Tips and strategy

  • Aim for the vertical center of the gap, not the top edge.
  • Two quick taps followed by a pause is often more stable than continuous tapping.
  • Look at the next pipe, not the bird — your hand will follow your eyes.
  • Restart immediately after a crash; the first pipe is always the same height.

Flappy Bird FAQ

Is this the original Flappy Bird?

This is a faithful HTML5 recreation built from scratch, not the original binary. It uses the same one-button rules, pipe layout and medal thresholds.

Can I play Flappy Bird unblocked at school?

Yes. The game is a static HTML5 page with no download or install, and it is designed to load quickly on school networks and Chromebooks.

What score do I need for a medal?

Bronze starts at 10 points, silver at 20, gold at 30 and platinum at 40. Your best medal is shown on the game-over screen.

Does Flappy Bird work with a touchscreen?

Yes. Tap anywhere on the game area to flap. The canvas is sized for both portrait phones and landscape Chromebooks.
